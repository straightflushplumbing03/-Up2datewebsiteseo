The direct answer

What is water leak detection?

Water leak detection uses specialized equipment — acoustic listening devices, electronic pipe locators, and thermal imaging cameras — to identify precisely where a leak is occurring inside walls, under floors, or underground, without needing to open up the entire area first.

It's the diagnostic step that should always happen before a repair quote. Skipping it is how homeowners end up with unnecessary drywall or slab demolition that misses the actual leak.

Signs you need it
  • Rising water bill with no change in usage
  • Water meter still spinning with everything off
  • Sound of running water behind a wall or under a floor
  • A warm spot on flooring (possible hot water slab leak)
  • Dropping water pressure
  • Damp carpet, bubbling floors, or a musty smell
Our technology

Three detection methods, matched to your situation

Acoustic

Sound-Based Detection

Sensitive listening equipment picks up the distinct sound of pressurized water escaping a pipe underground or inside a wall cavity.

Thermal

Infrared Imaging

Thermal cameras detect temperature differences caused by moisture or hot water leaks beneath flooring, invisible to the naked eye.

Electronic

Electronic Locating

Sensor-based tracing follows the exact path of a pipe, narrowing down the access point to a precise, small area.

Know when to call

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

A plumbing emergency is any situation where water is actively damaging your property, you cannot stop a leak, or a critical system has completely failed. The following situations warrant an immediate call.

Burst Pipe

A pipe that has ruptured can release hundreds of gallons of water in minutes. Shut off your main water supply immediately and call. Every minute counts.

Active Slab Leak

If you can hear water running constantly with everything off, feel a warm spot expanding on your floor, or see moisture wicking up through flooring, you likely have an active slab leak that needs same-day attention.

Major Water Leak

Any leak you cannot control with a shutoff valve, or one causing visible water damage to walls, ceilings, or floors, is an emergency. Mold growth begins within 24–48 hours.

Water Heater Failure

A water heater actively leaking or flooding the utility area needs immediate service. A failed tank can release significant water before the supply line is shut off.

No Water to the Home

A sudden complete loss of water pressure—with the street supply confirmed on—may indicate a major main line break. This requires diagnosis before damage progresses.

Sewer Backup

Sewage backing up into your home through floor drains or toilets is a health hazard and requires urgent attention. Do not use any drains until the blockage is cleared.

While you wait

What to do before the plumber arrives

Step 1

Shut Off the Water

Find your main shutoff valve—usually near the water meter or where the main line enters the home—and close it completely. This stops the majority of damage immediately.

Step 2

Stay Clear of Hazards

If water is near an electrical panel, outlets, or appliances, keep your distance and do not attempt repairs yourself. Water and electricity are a dangerous combination.

Step 3

Document the Damage

Take photos and video of the leak, standing water, and any visible damage before cleanup. This documentation is valuable for your insurance claim and helps us assess the situation on arrival.

Step 4

Call Straight Flush

Water heaters in Laguna Niguel homes

Much of Laguna Niguel was built in the 1970s–90s, from Bear Brand to Niguel Summit, and many of those homes still run their original copper plumbing. When we replace a water heater in a home of that age, we also look at the supply connectors, shutoff valve, and gas line feeding it, because older fittings are often the weak point — not the tank itself.

Hot water that runs out fast, or a warm spot on the floor, isn't always the heater. In copper-era homes on a slab, a leak on the hot-water line under the foundation can make a heater run constantly. Before we recommend a new unit, we rule that out with the same acoustic and thermal tools we use for slab leak detection.

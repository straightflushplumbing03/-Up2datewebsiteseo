 Our story


## Why the name "Straight Flush"



In poker, a straight flush is one of the strongest hands you can hold — every card accounted for, nothing left to chance. That's the standard we hold ourselves to on every job: know exactly what you're dealing with before you act.



Too many homeowners get a repair quote before anyone has actually confirmed where the leak is coming from. That approach leads to unnecessary demolition, repeat visits, and repairs that don't solve the real problem. We built Straight Flush around the opposite approach: diagnose thoroughly first, using real detection technology, then explain the options in plain language.



Based in Laguna Niguel and serving Dana Point, San Clemente, and the surrounding South Orange County communities, we treat every home like it's in our own neighborhood — because it usually is. **Lance answers the phone.** We are the specialist for [slab leaks in copper-era homes on expansive clay soils](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com//south-oc-slab-leak-specialist.html).

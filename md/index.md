## You may have a hidden leak if you've noticed…



Most hidden leaks don't announce themselves with a puddle. They show up as small, easy-to-dismiss signals first.



 1

**An unexplained jump in your water bill**Nothing in your routine changed, but the bill did.


 2

**Your water meter is still spinning**Every fixture is off, yet the meter keeps moving.


 3

**The sound of running water behind a wall**A faint hiss or trickle with no visible source.


 4

**A warm spot on the floor**Often a sign of a hot water slab leak underneath.


 5

**Low or dropping water pressure**A sudden change that wasn't there last month.


 6

**Damp carpet, bubbling floors, or musty smell**Moisture is finding its way somewhere it shouldn't.

## What homeowners ask before calling




 How do I know if I have a hidden water leak? +


Watch for a rising water bill with no change in usage, a water meter that keeps moving with everything off, warm spots on the floor, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are on. Any one of these is worth a professional diagnostic.




 Can a leak be found without damaging my home? +


In most cases, yes. Acoustic, electronic, and thermal detection let us narrow the leak to a small, specific area before any concrete or drywall is opened — which is the entire point of diagnosing before repairing.




 How long does leak detection take? +


Most residential leak detection visits take one to a few hours depending on the size of the home, the type of leak, and how accessible the plumbing is. We'll give you a time estimate up front.




 Does insurance cover slab leak detection? +


It depends entirely on your specific policy. We can't make coverage promises, but we can provide clear diagnostic findings and documentation to bring to your insurance company. See our [Insurance Resource Center](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com//insurance/) for more.




 What's the difference between repair, reroute, and repiping? +


A spot repair fixes a single, accessible leak. A reroute avoids a problem section of pipe entirely. Whole-house repiping replaces the system when age or repeat failures make that the smarter long-term option. We'll walk you through which fits your situation after diagnosis — never before.

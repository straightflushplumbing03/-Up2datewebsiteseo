## Water heaters in Rancho Santa Margarita homes



The first homes in the Rancho Santa Margarita planned community were sold in 1986, and the city incorporated on January 1, 2000 ([City of RSM General Plan](https://www.cityofrsm.org/DocumentCenter/View/5467/Introduction)). With tank heaters usually lasting 8 to 12 years, many houses here are on their second or third unit, but the gas line, vent, shutoff valve and connectors around it are often original. A replacement has to fit those connections and the space it sits in, whether that is a garage or an interior closet, and meet today's code. We check all of that before quoting, not after.



**The permit.** The City of Rancho Santa Margarita says a building permit is required to replace a water heater, and most water heater replacement permit applications can be completed over the counter ([City of RSM, Building & Safety FAQ](https://www.cityofrsm.org/m/faq?cat=18)). The permit comes from the City's Building & Safety Division at City Hall, 22112 El Paseo, not the County of Orange. The permit technician is at (949) 635-1800, ext. 6101, and inspections are requested at ext. 6100 ([City of RSM, Building & Safety](https://www.cityofrsm.org/196)). California also requires water heaters to be braced or strapped against earthquakes, and the inspector checks that along with the relief valve, venting and gas connection.



**Hard water.** The Santa Margarita Water District's 2026 water quality report lists average hardness in the district system at 256 mg/L, about 15 grains per gallon, with a range of 12.3 to 17.5 grains ([SMWD 2026 report](https://smwd.com/DocumentCenter/View/6349/2026-Water-Quality-Report)). That settles as sediment in a tank and as scale in a tankless heat exchanger, so flushing and descaling extend a unit's life. Eastern neighborhoods, including Robinson Ranch, Trabuco Highlands and Dove Canyon, are served by the Trabuco Canyon Water District instead ([City of RSM General Plan](https://www.cityofrsm.org/DocumentCenter/View/5467/Introduction)); your water bill names your district.

 Is my Rancho Santa Margarita water heater original to the house? +


It may be on its second or third. The first homes in the planned community were sold in 1986, so most houses are 25 to 40 years old, and tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years.  How long do water heaters last? +


Traditional tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years; tankless units can last considerably longer with regular descaling. Hard water and heavy use shorten that, so a unit's age is a guide, not a deadline.




 Is it the water heater or a hidden hot-water leak? +


Not always the heater. Running out of hot water, a gas or electric bill that climbs, a warm spot on the floor or the sound of running water can point to a leak on a hot-water line under the slab rather than the heater itself. We check for a hot-water leak before recommending a new unit.

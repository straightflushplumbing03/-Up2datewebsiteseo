## Water heaters in Aliso Viejo homes



Most Aliso Viejo homes were built in the 1980s and 1990s, and many are condos and townhomes where the water heater sits in a garage, a closet or a small utility space. A replacement has to fit that space, its venting and its gas and water connections, and older connectors or seismic strapping often need updating to meet current California requirements.



Traditional tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years; tankless units can last considerably longer with regular descaling. Hard water speeds up sediment in tanks and scale in tankless units. Most of the city is served by Moulton Niguel Water District and part by El Toro Water District; your district's annual water quality report shows your water hardness.



In a condo or townhome, a leaking heater can damage a neighbor's unit as well as yours. If water is pooling near the heater, shut off the cold-water supply valve on top of the tank and call.  How long do water heaters last in Aliso Viejo homes? +


Traditional tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years; tankless units can last considerably longer with regular descaling. Hard water and heavy use shorten that, so a unit's age is a guide, not a deadline.




 My condo water heater is leaking. What do I do first? +


Shut off the cold-water supply valve on top of the heater, turn the gas control to off or switch off the breaker for an electric unit, and keep water away from walls shared with a neighbor. Then call to have the leak diagnosed: sometimes it is a valve or connector, not the tank.




 Is tankless worth it in an Aliso Viejo townhome? +


It can be, but it depends on your gas line size, where the unit can be vented, and how long you'll stay. We'll walk through the real trade-offs for your home before you commit.




 The water heater looks fine but I hear running water. What is it? +


If the heater is dry but you hear water running with everything off or feel a warm spot on the floor, a hot-water line under the slab may be leaking. That calls for leak detection, not a new heater.

 The direct answer


## What is water leak detection?



Water leak detection uses specialized equipment — acoustic listening devices, electronic pipe locators, and thermal imaging cameras — to identify precisely where a leak is occurring inside walls, under floors, or underground, without needing to open up the entire area first.



It's the diagnostic step that should always happen before a repair quote. Skipping it is how homeowners end up with unnecessary drywall or slab demolition that misses the actual leak.



 Signs you need it



- Rising water bill with no change in usage

- Water meter still spinning with everything off

- Sound of running water behind a wall or under a floor

- A warm spot on flooring (possible hot water slab leak)

- Dropping water pressure

- Damp carpet, bubbling floors, or a musty smell









 Our technology


## Three detection methods, matched to your situation




 Acoustic


### Sound-Based Detection



Sensitive listening equipment picks up the distinct sound of pressurized water escaping a pipe underground or inside a wall cavity.



 Thermal


### Infrared Imaging



Thermal cameras detect temperature differences caused by moisture or hot water leaks beneath flooring, invisible to the naked eye.



 Electronic


### Electronic Locating



Sensor-based tracing follows the exact path of a pipe, narrowing down the access point to a precise, small area.

## Common questions



 How do you find a leak without cutting into walls or floors? +


We isolate the system with pressure testing, then use acoustic listening equipment to hear the sound of escaping water, thermal imaging to spot temperature changes from hot-water leaks, and electronic locating to trace the pipe's path. That narrows the leak to a small, specific area before any wall, floor, or concrete is opened, so any access needed stays as small as possible.




 What is the difference between leak detection and slab leak detection? +


General leak detection covers leaks anywhere in the system, including walls, ceilings, fixtures, and under floors. Slab leak detection is the same diagnostic approach focused on pressurized water lines running beneath a concrete foundation, where acoustic and thermal tools pinpoint the leak without breaking up the slab.




 Will my insurance cover leak detection? +


Coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the loss. Many policies address the resulting water damage rather than the plumbing repair itself. We document what we find so you have clear evidence for a claim, and our insurance resources cover what to ask your provider.

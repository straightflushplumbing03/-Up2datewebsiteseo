 Should I repair or replace my water heater in an older Laguna Niguel home? +


It depends on the unit's age and what failed. A thermostat, element or valve is usually worth repairing; a leaking tank or a unit past roughly 8 to 12 years usually isn't. We diagnose first and tell you which one makes sense — including when a repair is all you need.




 My hot water runs out fast — is it the water heater or a leak? +


It can be either. Sediment or a failing element is common, but in Laguna Niguel's copper-era slab homes a leak on the hot-water line under the floor can also make the heater run nonstop. We check for both before recommending a new unit.

## Common questions



 Who does drain cleaning and hydro jetting in South Orange County? +


Straight Flush Plumbing & Leak Detection clears clogged drains and main sewer lines across South Orange County, including hydro jetting and sewer camera inspection. We're an owner-operated plumbing company based in Laguna Niguel; call owner Lance at [(949) 374-6524](tel:+19493746524).




 Is the sewer line under my yard and sidewalk my responsibility? +


Usually, yes. Orange County sewer agencies' guide for property owners says owners maintain the private sewer lateral from the home to the connection with the public sewer, and that this can include areas under the sidewalk and street. The public sewer main is the sewer agency's job. If sewage is coming from a manhole or a neighbor has the same problem, call your sewer agency first.




 What should I do if sewage is backing up into my house? +


Stop using water and turn off the main water supply, keep people and pets away from the area, and call a plumber to clear the blockage. If sewage is reaching a gutter or storm drain, report it to your sewer agency or the Orange County 24-hour pollution hotline at 1-877-89-SPILL (1-877-897-7455); owners can face fines if sewage from their property reaches a storm drain.




 Do I need a camera inspection for a slow drain? +


Not always. A single, isolated slow drain is often solved with standard cleaning. Camera inspection becomes valuable when the problem is recurring, affects multiple fixtures, or involves the main sewer line.




 What causes a main sewer line backup? +


Common causes include tree root intrusion, aging or collapsed clay pipe, grease buildup, and, in older Orange County neighborhoods, simple pipe age.




 Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes? +


It can be, but pipe age and material matter. We'll assess the line's condition first and recommend the appropriate cleaning method rather than defaulting to the most aggressive option.

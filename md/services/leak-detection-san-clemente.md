## Where leaks show up in San Clemente homes



**Copper lines in newer tracts, not just old homes.** In 2015 a class action was announced alleging that copper pipe installed in San Clemente-area subdivisions, including Talega, was prone to leaks ([PHCP Pros](https://phcppros.com/articles/657-lawsuit-blames-california-home-builders-for-leaks-from-copper-pipes)). Whatever the cause in a given house, a first pinhole leak is a reason to check the rest of the system.



**Under the slab.** Many San Clemente homes sit on concrete slabs with water lines running underneath. A warm spot on the floor or the sound of running water with everything off are typical signs. See [how we find slab leaks](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/services/slab-leak-detection.html).



**Inside walls and ceilings.** Supply lines to bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms run behind drywall, and in two-story homes a leak upstairs often first appears as a ceiling stain below.



**Between the meter and the house, and on sloped lots.** The buried service line and irrigation lines can leak for a long time with no sign indoors except the bill. A soggy patch, or a spot that stays green in a dry month, can point to it.



## Check for a leak yourself first



Turn off every faucet, appliance and irrigation zone, note the reading on your water meter, and check again after 30 minutes. If it moved, water is leaving the system somewhere.  How we work


## Find the leak first, then a straight answer on the fix



 1 · Isolate

### Which line is leaking?

Pressure testing shows whether the hot line, cold line or the service line from the meter is losing water, so we know where to look.


 2 · Pinpoint

### Listen, trace and confirm

Acoustic equipment picks up the sound of pressurized water escaping, electronic locating traces where the pipe runs, and thermal imaging cross-checks hot-water leaks. The spot is marked for the smallest possible opening.


 3 · Decide

### Repair, reroute, or repipe

A single leak in a sound line is usually a spot repair. Repeat leaks in aging or failing copper may make a reroute or repipe the smarter call. We explain the trade-offs after diagnosis, never before.

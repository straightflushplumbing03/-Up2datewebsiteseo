## Repiping in Dana Point homes



A number of older Dana Point homes near the harbor still have original copper plumbing, and salt air and coastal humidity are hard on any exposed pipe, valve or fitting. Corrosion at several fixtures, pinhole leaks, or discolored water are the usual reasons a homeowner starts asking about a repipe.



PEX doesn't corrode the way copper does, and its flexible runs need fewer fittings behind walls and under slabs. That removes the most common cause of repeat leaks in older homes — though no plumbing system is leak-proof forever.



A repipe doesn't have to be all or nothing. On a recent Dana Point job we [replaced the copper at a kitchen island with PEX and rebuilt 18 shutoff valves](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/case-studies/dana-point-kitchen-pex-shutoff-valves.html) instead of repiping the whole house.

## Water heaters in Laguna Hills homes



Laguna Hills grew out of the Moulton Ranch, which was subdivided in the early 1960s, and became a city on December 20, 1991 ([City of Laguna Hills, History](https://lagunahillsca.gov/247/History-of-Laguna-Hills)). The median home was built in 1980, and about 24% of homes are multi-family ([City-Stats, 2023 ACS data](https://city-stats.com/california/laguna-hills/housing)). Many heaters here sit in a garage or a closet that was built around a 1970s or 1980s unit, so a replacement has to fit the existing gas line, vent and water connections and meet today's code. In a condo or townhome, the closet, the vent route and the drain for the relief valve can be tighter still. We check all of that before quoting, not after.



The City of Laguna Hills Building Division publishes a residential water heater handout drawn from the 2022 California Plumbing Code ([City of Laguna Hills, Water Heater](https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/7834/Water-Heater)). It calls for approved earthquake strapping in the top and bottom thirds of the heater, with the bottom strap at least 4 inches above the controls; a temperature and pressure relief valve piped to the outside, ending 6 to 24 inches above grade and facing down; an expansion tank where the system has a check valve, backflow preventer or other normally closed device; and, in a garage, the burner at least 18 inches above the floor. A heater that is moved to a new spot must meet all current code requirements.



Water hardness depends on your district. The City lists El Toro Water District and Moulton Niguel Water District as serving Laguna Hills residents ([City of Laguna Hills](https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/7819/Outside-Agency-Contact-List)). Moulton Niguel's 2023 water quality report gives an average hardness of 13.3 grains per gallon ([MNWD 2023 report](https://mnwd.com/2023ccr)); El Toro customers can find their figure in the district's annual water quality report. Hard water settles as sediment in a tank and as scale in a tankless heat exchanger, so flushing and descaling extend a unit's life.

 Is Laguna Hills water hard on water heaters? +

 How long do water heaters last? +


Traditional tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years; tankless units can last considerably longer with regular descaling. Hard water and heavy use shorten that, so a unit's age is a guide, not a deadline.




 Is it the water heater or a hidden hot-water leak? +


Not always the heater. Running out of hot water, a gas or electric bill that climbs, a warm spot on the floor or the sound of running water can point to a leak on a hot-water line under the slab rather than the heater itself. With the median Laguna Hills home built in 1980, we check for a hot-water leak before recommending a new unit.

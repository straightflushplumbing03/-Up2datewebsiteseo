## Repiping in Mission Viejo homes



Mission Viejo was built as a master-planned community starting in the mid-1960s, and many homes in the older tracts still run on their original copper supply lines. After decades in service, repeat pinhole leaks, a second slab leak, corroded fittings or discolored water are the usual reasons a homeowner starts asking about a repipe.



PEX doesn't corrode the way copper does, and its flexible runs need fewer fittings behind walls and under slabs. That removes the most common cause of repeat leaks in older homes, though no plumbing system is leak-proof forever.



A repipe doesn't have to be all or nothing. If the trouble is limited to one area or line, replacing that section or rerouting a line is often enough. We tell you which one your home needs after diagnosis.

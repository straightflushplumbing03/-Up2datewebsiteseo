 Is Mission Viejo water hard on water heaters? +


Often, yes. Moulton Niguel Water District, one of three districts serving Mission Viejo, reported an average hardness of 13.3 grains per gallon in its 2023 water quality report and describes its water as hard. Hard water leaves sediment in tank heaters and scale in tankless units, so flushing and descaling matter more here. If your bill comes from Santa Margarita or El Toro Water District, check that district's annual report for your figure.




 How long do water heaters last in Mission Viejo homes? +


Traditional tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years; tankless units can last considerably longer with regular descaling. Hard water and heavy use shorten that, so a unit's age is a guide, not a deadline.




 Does a replacement water heater have to be strapped in California? +


Yes. California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires all new and replacement water heaters, and existing residential water heaters, to be braced, anchored or strapped to resist falling or moving in an earthquake. Older Mission Viejo garages sometimes have straps that no longer meet current requirements, and we include strapping when we replace the unit.

 Is it the water heater or a hidden hot-water leak? +


Not always the heater. Running out of hot water, a gas or electric bill that climbs, a warm spot on the floor or the sound of running water can point to a leak on a hot-water line under the slab rather than the heater itself. With many Mission Viejo homes now 40 to 60 years old, we check for a hot-water slab leak before recommending a new unit.

## Repiping Laguna Niguel's copper-era homes



Much of Laguna Niguel was built in the 1970s–90s, and many of those homes still run their original copper water lines. Copper tends to wear throughout a system at the same rate, so when one section of an older line pinholes, other sections of the same age are often not far behind.



That's the point where homeowners start asking about a repipe: two or more slab leaks within a few years, corrosion at several fixtures, discolored water, or simply being tired of patching the same system. PEX resists the corrosion that causes those pinhole leaks, and its flexible runs need fewer fittings — fewer joints means fewer places for a future leak to start.



Not every home needs the whole system replaced. Sometimes a reroute of one problem line solves it. We test and inspect first, then tell you which applies — see [repair vs. reroute vs. repipe](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/guides/repair-vs-reroute-vs-repipe.html).

 Is PEX better than copper for older Laguna Niguel homes? +


For homes with aging copper, PEX is usually the better long-term choice: it doesn't corrode the way copper does, and its flexible runs need fewer fittings, which means fewer places for a leak to start. It's also certified for drinking water and standard in new residential construction.

 Signs you need service


## When to call about your water heater



 1

**No hot water, or it runs out fast**A failing heating element or sediment buildup is often the cause.


 2

**Rumbling or popping sounds**Usually sediment hardening at the bottom of the tank.


 3

**Water pooling around the base**Could be a tank leak — worth an inspection before it worsens.


 4

**Discolored hot water**Often a sign of corrosion inside an aging tank.


 5

**Your unit is 8–12+ years old**Most tank water heaters are nearing the end of their service life by then.


 6

**Rising energy bills**An inefficient or failing unit can quietly cost you more every month.

 Water heater FAQ


## Common questions



 Is it worth switching to tankless? +


It depends on your household's hot water usage, existing gas/electric setup, and how long you plan to stay in the home. We'll walk through the real numbers with you rather than a blanket recommendation.




 How long do water heaters typically last? +


Traditional tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years, while tankless units can last considerably longer with proper maintenance.




 Can a water heater leak cause a slab leak? +


A failing water heater can leak at its base or connections, but a true slab leak involves a pipe under the foundation. If you're not sure which you're dealing with, we can diagnose it directly.

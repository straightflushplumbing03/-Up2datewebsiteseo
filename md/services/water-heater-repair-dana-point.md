## Water heaters in Dana Point homes



Salt air and coastal humidity are hard on anything metal that's exposed — and many Dana Point water heaters sit in garages or exterior closets. We check the gas connection, venting, shutoff valve and supply connectors for corrosion, not just the tank, because a corroded fitting can fail before the heater does.



A number of older homes near the harbor have original plumbing. If a heater is leaking at its base, we confirm whether the water is coming from the tank, its connections, or a nearby line before recommending anything — guesswork tends to cost more here than elsewhere.



California requires water heaters to be strapped against earthquake movement. When we replace a unit, we install it with proper seismic strapping and bring the connections up to current requirements.

 Does salt air shorten the life of a water heater in Dana Point? +


Coastal air mainly attacks exposed metal — gas connectors, vent pipes, valves and fittings — especially on units in garages or exterior closets. Regular checks of those parts can catch corrosion before it causes a leak or a venting problem.




 My water heater is leaking at the bottom — do I need a new one? +


Not always. Water at the base can come from the relief valve, a loose connection or a nearby pipe rather than the tank. A leaking tank does need replacement, so we confirm the source before quoting either.




 Do you install tankless water heaters in Dana Point? +


Yes. We repair tankless units and handle tank-to-tankless conversions, including checking gas supply and venting, and we'll go over whether tankless makes sense for your home and how long you plan to stay.

# How much does leak detection cost?



Leak detection for most Orange County homes runs $365 to $525. Where your home falls in that range depends on its size, the type of leak, and how accessible the plumbing is.

 [Get A Straight Answer](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/contact.html)





 The direct answer


## What affects the price




- Size of the home and number of suspect areas

- Whether the leak is a slab leak, wall leak, or underground line

- How accessible the plumbing is (finished floors, landscaping, etc.)

- Which detection methods are needed — acoustic, thermal, or electronic

- Whether documentation is needed for an insurance claim

 What you're paying for


## What a leak detection test includes



A leak detection test is a diagnostic visit, not a repair. The price covers the time and equipment it takes to find the leak's exact location without opening walls or floors to look.




- Confirming there is a leak, using the water meter and a pressure test on the supply lines

- Narrowing it to one line: hot or cold, inside the slab, inside a wall, or in the yard

- Pinpointing the spot with [acoustic listening](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/academy/acoustic-leak-detection-explained.html), [thermal imaging](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/academy/thermal-imaging-leak-detection-explained.html) or [electronic pipe locating](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/academy/electronic-pipe-locating-explained.html), depending on the leak

- Walking you through the options on the spot: repair, reroute or repipe

- Photos and notes for an insurance claim, when you need them




Most detection visits take one to a few hours. ## Common questions



 Is leak detection expensive compared to just guessing and repairing? +


Usually not, once you factor in the cost of repairs that miss the actual leak. Accurate detection prevents paying to open the wrong wall or floor twice.




 Will you give me a price over the phone? +


We can give you a general range based on what you describe, but a firm number comes after understanding your specific situation — that's the same diagnose-first standard we apply to the plumbing itself.




 Does homeowners insurance ever cover the cost of detection? +


If you're filing an insurance claim, insurance typically reimburses the leak detection cost. Coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the leak, so see our Insurance Resource Center for what to check with your provider.




 How much do plumbers charge to find a leak? +


For most Orange County homes, leak detection runs $365 to $525. Where you fall depends on the size of the home, how many areas need testing, and whether the leak is under the slab, inside a wall, or underground. We'll give you a clear number before we schedule the visit, so there are no surprise charges.




 Is a leak detection test the same as a repair? +


No. The test finds the leak's exact location. Repair is priced separately, after you've seen where the leak is and chosen between a spot repair, a reroute, or a repipe.




 Why do leak detection prices vary between companies? +


Companies use different methods. A quick look and a guess costs less up front than acoustic, thermal and pressure testing, but a guess can lead to opening the wrong wall or floor.

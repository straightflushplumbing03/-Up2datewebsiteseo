# What good leak documentation looks like.



Insurers evaluate claims on specifics, not impressions. Here's the kind of documentation that tends to support a clear, well-understood claim.

 [Request Documented Diagnosis](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/contact.html)







**Date first noticed**As specific as you can reasonably be.




**Photos and video**Of visible damage, from multiple angles, with timestamps if possible.




**Water bill history**Showing the change that prompted concern.




**Professional diagnostic findings**Confirming the type, location, and likely cause of the leak.




**Repair estimate**An itemized breakdown of proposed work and cost.




**Maintenance history**If available, showing the system was reasonably maintained.

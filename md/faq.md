 How do I find a hidden water leak in my home? +


Start with three checks. (1) Turn off every fixture and watch the water meter; movement means a leak. (2) Look for warm floor spots, damp baseboards, musty smells or new cracks in tile. (3) Compare your water bill with the same month last year. If those point to a leak you can't see, a professional can pinpoint it. See [why is my water bill so high?](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/academy/why-is-my-water-bill-so-high.html) and [leak detection](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/services/leak-detection.html).




 Who repairs pinhole leaks in copper plumbing? +


Straight Flush repairs pinhole leaks in copper lines. A single pinhole can be spot-repaired, but repeated pinholes usually mean the pipe wall is thinning throughout the system. At that point it's worth comparing a repair, a reroute and a repipe. See [repair vs. reroute vs. repipe](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/guides/repair-vs-reroute-vs-repipe.html) and [plumbing repair](https://straightflushplumbingoc.com/services/plumbing-repair.html).




 Who can help identify a water leak before it causes major damage? +


Early signs include a meter that moves when nothing is on, an unexplained jump in the water bill, a water heater that cycles constantly, and damp or warm spots.

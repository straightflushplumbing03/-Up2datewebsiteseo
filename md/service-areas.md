 Service area FAQ


## Straight answers about where we work



 What areas does Straight Flush Plumbing serve? +


Laguna Niguel (home base) and communities throughout South and Central Orange County — every city with a page in the grid above, plus surrounding neighborhoods.




 Do you serve a city that isn't listed on this page? +


Often yes — we regularly take calls from nearby Orange County communities that don't have their own page yet. Call [(949) 374-6524](tel:+19493746524) and ask; Lance answers the phone and will confirm whether your address is in range.




 Is there a difference in service between cities? +


The diagnose-first standard is identical everywhere. What changes by city is the housing stock — 1970s–1990s copper-era homes in Laguna Niguel, Mission Viejo, and Aliso Viejo have different slab leak patterns than newer developments or the historic homes in San Juan Capistrano and Orange.




 How fast can you reach South Orange County addresses? +


Most South OC cities are within a short drive of our Laguna Niguel base, and emergency calls are prioritized. Regular service is scheduled in visit windows confirmed up front.

Slab leaks

Slab Leak Detection in Laguna Niguel

A slab leak is a failure in a water line that runs through or beneath a home's concrete foundation. Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with everything shut off, a spike in the water or gas bill, or unexplained moisture at the base of a wall are the reasons homeowners call us to investigate. Not every one of those symptoms turns out to be a slab leak, and that is precisely why the diagnostic step matters.

Slab leaks come up often enough in Laguna Niguel that they are worth understanding before you need the information. The plumbing here is largely built out from the 1960s through the 1990s, and the most common call we take is copper supply-line failures in 1970s and 1980s subdivisions.

Water lines

Water Line Leak Detection & Repair

Water service lines and pressurized supply lines carry water under constant pressure, so a failure can lose a significant volume of water before it ever reaches the surface. Underground service lines between the meter and the house are a common source, particularly where soil movement or age has stressed the pipe.

In Laguna Niguel the supply side is worth ruling out early, because a pressurized line loses water continuously and quietly. That distinguishes it from a drain problem, which usually only shows when something is running.

The useful first step is establishing whether the loss is on the pressurized supply side or the drainage side, and whether it is inside the structure or outside it. Straight Flush diagnoses pressurized water line leaks, including underground lines, before recommending repair or rerouting. Related: plumbing repair and emergency plumbing.

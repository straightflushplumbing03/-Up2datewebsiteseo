What we know about Foothill Ranch homes

Homes throughout Foothill Ranch benefit from routine attention to water pressure and early leak detection, especially as the community's original construction continues to age.

Foothill Ranch is a master-planned neighborhood in the northeast corner of Lake Forest (ZIP 92610), on land that was once Whiting Ranch. Hon Development bought almost 3,000 acres of the ranch in 1987, the County of Orange adopted the Foothill Ranch Planned Community plan on April 20, 1988 (Foothill Ranch Planned Community text), and building began in 1989 with the first homes for sale in 1990. Nearly all of the initial construction was finished by 1999, and today the community has over 14,000 residents in almost 5,000 households, next to the 2,500-acre Whiting Ranch Wilderness Park (Foothill Ranch Maintenance Association). It was a census-designated place until it was annexed to Lake Forest in 2000 (Wikipedia).

That compact 1990s build-out means most original plumbing here is roughly 25 to 35 years old, the age when water heaters, valves, supply lines and under-slab pipes begin to wear. Because Foothill Ranch is inside the City of Lake Forest, plumbing permits go through the City’s Building and Safety Division. Water comes from a public district, not the City: Lake Forest lists Irvine Ranch Water District for most residents, plus El Toro and Trabuco Canyon water districts for other areas (City of Lake Forest utilities). Check the name on your water bill to confirm your provider.

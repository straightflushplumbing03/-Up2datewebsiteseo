Water main leak found near the house shutoff — Rancho Santa Margarita

A leak on the incoming water line of a Rancho Santa Margarita home, located without digging blind, October 2026.

Schedule Service
The problem

What the homeowner was dealing with

The homeowner suspected a leak on the water service. We tested the main supply line that runs from the water meter to the shutoff valve at the house.

The job

What we did in this Rancho Santa Margarita home

  1. Diagnostic testing on the water main. Tested and evaluated the line from the meter to the house shutoff to narrow down where water was escaping.
  2. Leak located. The findings put the leak about 1 to 2 feet from the house-side main shutoff valve, on the incoming supply line.
  3. Repair options explained. Given where the leak sat and the age of the existing pipe, we recommended replacing the whole line from the meter to the house rather than patching one spot.
Why it matters

Why we recommended replacing the line, not patching it

When an older water main leaks once, the rest of the same pipe has usually aged the same way. A spot repair fixes today’s leak but leaves the next one waiting. A new continuous line from the meter to the house removes that risk and the cost of digging twice. Excavation, permits and any concrete work are quoted separately once the homeowner decides.
